## What's included

A free workspace includes the whole platform. The first project is usually a folder, a notebook, and a few sources — add the rest as you need it.

## Create your first project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace.">
    Pick a name you'll recognize later, like the study or lab you're working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first files.">
    Upload a CSV, a notebook, or a PDF. Spindle detects common formats and previews them in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the lab.">
    Start RStudio, JupyterLab, VS Code, or a terminal on the same files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a search.">
    Save results and citations into the project instead of a browser history.
  </Step>
</Steps>
