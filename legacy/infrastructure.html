01 THE CONNECTION

A protected line, every time you visit.

When you open Spindle, your browser sends an HTTPS request to our servers. TLS encrypts it in transit, Cloudflare authenticates it at the edge, and only then does it reach the workspace. There is no open-backdoor path to our origin.

  1. Your browser opens the request.HTTPS negotiates a TLS session before any project data moves.
  2. Cloudflare verifies it.The edge terminates TLS, checks the request, and applies our rules.
  3. You get the website.Verified traffic continues; suspicious traffic never reaches Spindle.
  4. The origin stays hidden.Cloudflare Tunnels connect outward from our servers, so no inbound port has to be exposed.

Bots stop at the door. Attacks stop at the edge.

Cloudflare sits in front of Spindle, so hostile traffic is filtered long before it can touch a workspace. Your projects stay reachable without exposing our servers to the internet.

02 THE STORAGE

A private vault for your files and projects.

Spindle stores your files and project data in Backblaze B2, durable object storage built for keeping valuable data safe. Buckets stay private. Access is scoped to Spindle's services. Nothing in your workspace becomes a public link by accident.

  • Private buckets onlyNo open-to-the-internet objects and no guessed URLs into your project.
  • Encrypted in transit and at restTLS on the way in, server-side encryption once the object is stored.
  • Scoped access, not shared secretsOnly the service that needs an object can read or write it.
  • Made to lastRedundant object storage keeps research intact across projects and seasons.
Your files are never used to train ad models, broker profiles, or mine your research. They are stored so you can keep working — nothing more.
