SQL over your files. No server required.

The SQL Explorer is powered by duckdb-wasm — DuckDB compiled to WebAssembly and running right in your browser. Query CSV, Parquet, and in-memory tables with full SQL, and the data never has to leave your machine.

WHY DUCKDB-WASM

Columnar scans, joins, and aggregations happen locally at near-native speed. There is no database to provision, no upload step, and no wait — point a query at a project file and get results instantly.
